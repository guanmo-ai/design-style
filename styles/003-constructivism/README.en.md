## Overview

A diagonal, a splash of red, and type that shouts. This 15-second film follows two axes (−30° and 60°) through wedges, posters, a tower, textiles, stage sets, and books.

Series: **Design styles**. Period shown in this episode: **1917—1932**.

## Five practical rules

| Rule | How to apply it |
|---|---|
| **Two axes** | Use a −30° main axis and a perpendicular 60° secondary axis. Keep explanatory text horizontal. |
| **Red and black** | Use red and black on warm paper; reserve red for the main action. |
| **Construction** | Assemble wedges, circles, and heavy bars as connected parts. Keep corners sharp. |
| **Photomontage** | Crop a photograph to one important detail, then combine it with geometry. |
| **Loud type** | Scale the key word boldly, even beyond the frame. Use real letters rather than fake Cyrillic. |

## Common pitfalls

Avoid arbitrary tilts, rounded corners, gradients, and fake Cyrillic lettering. The episode studies design methods and uses reinterpreted references rather than reproducing political slogans.
