# New Wave Typography · Making version two

The cover turned into a light table: letters, halftones, line screens and colors are separate sheets. They can slide, overlap and slip out of register. The eight scenes move through the cover, color overlays, stepped type, tracking, grid displacement, halftones, six layout examples and the final registration snap.

Version two uses “Minimalist Design Pulse” with short pitched sound effects. The Canvas timeline follows a 130.07 BPM grid with beat zero at frame 3.96. Beat 8 pushes into the stepped type, beat 24 grows halftones to black, and beat 39 lands the magenta bar and halftone ripple under the signature.

The film was rendered frame by frame at 1920×1080 and 60 fps. Fast pushes and camera moves sample several instants within a frame for motion blur. The gallery uses all 1,200 frames and the soundtrack from the version-two master.
