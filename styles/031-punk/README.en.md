# Punk Xerox

An 18-second motion study of punk zines and photocopied flyers. Cut-out lettering, tape, and fluorescent stock form the opening cover. The camera dives into a halftone dot, emerges inside a zine, and pulls back to a wall of gig posters.

## Four photocopier techniques

| Technique | On screen |
| --- | --- |
| Threshold | A brightness cutoff splits grayscale artwork into ink and paper. The feeder cycles through a microphone, guitar, amplifier, and crowd. |
| Halftone | Dots, lines, squares, and crosses rebuild tone. The screen angle rotates from 45° to 15°. |
| Copy generations | Fine strokes break and shadows clump as the same page passes through successive copies, from generation 1 to 64. |
| Paper stock | The ink stays black while the poster changes between pink, yellow, white, and green stock. |
