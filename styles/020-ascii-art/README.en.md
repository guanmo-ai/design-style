# 020 · ASCII Art

A spinning donut relays into digital rain, a terminal website, a phone player and character fire. Five rules and five phone states turn the look into reusable motion recipes.

[Watch the film](https://guanmo-ai.github.io/design-style/?lang=en#work-020-ascii-art) · [Film and full source](https://github.com/guanmo-ai/design-style/releases/tag/day-20) · [Production overview](PROCESS.en.md) · [中文](README.md)

## Visual rules

1. Map luminance to character density.
2. Use a fixed 0.6em × 1em monospaced cell.
3. Drive glyph and colour with the same light value.
4. Keep cells fixed while changing their glyphs.
5. Resolve scrambled text into the actual final label.

## Interface applications

The last section demonstrates loading, buttons, skeleton screens, typing and completion. These are motion studies: connect them to real application state and offer a reduced-motion version when reusing them.

## Editable illustrations

Four code-generated SVG sheets cover the poster, rules, phone applications and a four-step tutorial. Editable text and paths are in `images/`; self-contained font subsets are in `images/preview/`; double-resolution exports are in `images/png/`.
