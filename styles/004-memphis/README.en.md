## Overview

One color per shape, a black outline, and a playful tilt. Candy-colored blocks drop, squash, and bounce through a record, the Carlton bookcase, Tahiti lamp, First chair, patterns, and a modern app in 15 seconds.

Series: **Design styles**. Period shown in this episode: **1980—1988**.

## Five practical rules

| Rule | How to apply it |
|---|---|
| **Geometric blocks** | Build from circles, triangles, squares, half circles, and squiggles. |
| **Candy colors** | Use one flat color per block and separate neighboring colors clearly. |
| **Patterns** | Use restrained black patterns on broad areas; keep readable text on a quiet background. |
| **Playful tilt** | Tilt decorative objects by 6°, 12°, or 18°. Keep dates, prices, and body text level. |
| **Black outlines** | Unify the blocks with black outlines and hard offset shadows without blur. |

## Common pitfalls

Playful composition still needs clear boundaries and readable information. Keep soft shadows, glow, and random neon decoration out of this particular treatment.
