## Overview

More than red, yellow, and blue. A 15-second film explores nine sides of Bauhaus, from color and typography to architecture and the stage.

Series: **Design styles**. Period shown in this episode: **1919—1933**.

## Five practical rules

| Rule | How to apply it |
|---|---|
| **Basic shapes** | Build with circles, squares, triangles, and their slices. Start with two to four shapes. |
| **Primary colors** | Use flat red, yellow, blue, black, and paper. Give one primary color the leading role. |
| **Geometric type** | Use bold geometric sans-serif type; this episode uses lowercase Latin headlines. |
| **Asymmetric balance** | Balance large and small shapes with color and negative space rather than centering everything. |
| **Grid and rules** | Align edges to a grid. Use strong black dividers when structure needs to be visible. |

## Common pitfalls

These five rules describe the visual direction of this episode, not every work made at the Bauhaus. The school also worked across textiles, ceramics, metal, photography, architecture, and the stage.
