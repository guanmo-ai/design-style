# Layout 03 · Making Honeycomb

[中文](PROCESS.md) · **English** · [Layout guide](README.en.md)

## One grid across the film

The same cell centers carry the sequence from icons to photos, amber cells, a blueprint, a watch, a phone, and a hexbin map. The fisheye compresses positions toward the center and scales cells using the slope of the position curve. A central region stays full size.

## Version four

The film returns to 18 seconds and adds two scenes, a three-tone hexagon rotation and a mobile interest picker. The grid shrinks into the watch. The watch stretches into a phone, where taps, spring motion, and checkmarks show the selection.

Each scene has one focal line of type, such as 120°, 1:1, or 0.17s. Type slides in through a mask while nearby cells make room for it. The synth score runs at 120 BPM, with separate cues for flips, snaps, and the final seven-cell reveal.

## Film and source

The release uses the version-four original at 1920×1080, 60 fps, and 1,080 frames. The [timeline](process/timeline.json) covers ten scenes and the closing sequence. The [source directory](video/README.md) links to the original storyboard, contact sheet, Canvas renderer, and audio scripts.
